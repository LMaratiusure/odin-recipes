Dumplings

Description

Dumplings are small pieces of dough filled with meat, vegetables, or other
ingredients. They can be boiled, steamed, fried, or baked. Dumplings are popular
in many cultures and are often served with sauce or soup.

Ingredients

Dough

  • 2 cups all-purpose flour
  • ¾ cup warm water
  • ½ teaspoon salt

Filling

  • 1 pound ground meat, such as beef, chicken, pork, or lamb
  • 1 small onion, finely chopped
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 cup cabbage, finely chopped
  • 1 tablespoon soy sauce
  • 1 teaspoon salt
  • ½ teaspoon black pepper

Optional

  • Green onions
  • Ginger
  • Sesame oil
  • Dipping sauce, such as soy sauce or vinegar

Steps

  1. Mix flour, salt, and warm water in a bowl to make the dough.
  2. Knead the dough for 5–7 minutes until it becomes smooth.
  3. Cover the dough and let it rest for about 30 minutes.
  4. In another bowl, mix ground meat, onion, garlic, cabbage, soy sauce, salt, and black pepper.
  5. Roll the dough into a thin sheet.
  6. Cut the dough into small circles.
  7. Place a spoonful of filling in the center of each circle.
  8. Fold the dough over the filling and seal the edges.
  9. Boil, steam, or pan-fry the dumplings until fully cooked.
  10. Serve warm with soy sauce, vinegar, or your favorite dipping sauce.
